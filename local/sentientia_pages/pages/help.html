Find answers to common questions about using airpay academy.

Getting Started

Log in with your credentials and visit the Dashboard. You'll see your enrolled courses, deadlines, and progress. Browse the Catalog to discover new learning opportunities.

Taking Courses

Click any course to begin learning. Your progress saves automatically. Complete all modules and pass the assessment to earn a verifiable digital certificate.

Certificates

Certificates are issued automatically on course completion. Download as PDF or share directly to your LinkedIn profile from your Profile page.

Account & Access

Use "Forgot Password" on the login page to reset your password. For account issues, contact academy@airpay.co.in with your registered email address.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course?

Browse the Catalog, select a course, and click "Enroll". Most courses for registered users are free. Some premium courses may require payment through the Airpay payment gateway.

Can I retake an assessment?

Yes, most assessments allow unlimited retakes and your highest score is recorded. Some compliance exams may have a cooldown period between attempts.

Is there a mobile app?

airpay academy is a progressive web app (PWA). On mobile, visit the site in Chrome and tap "Add to Home Screen" for an app-like experience with offline support.

How do I contact support?

Email academy@airpay.co.in with your query. Include screenshots if reporting a technical issue. We respond within 24 hours on business days (Mon-Fri, 10 AM - 6 PM IST).
