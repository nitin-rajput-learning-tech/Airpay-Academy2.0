How can we help you?

Getting Started

New to Airpay Academy? Start with your Dashboard to see assigned courses, track progress, and explore the course catalog.

Taking Courses

Click on any course card to start learning. Progress is saved automatically. Complete all modules and pass the assessment to earn your certificate.

Certificates

Certificates are issued automatically upon course completion. View and download them from your Profile page.

Password Issues

Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. If your account is locked, contact your HR Business Partner or the L&D team.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find mandatory training?

Mandatory courses appear on your Dashboard with deadline badges. You can also check the Catalog and filter by "Compliance" category.

Can I retake an assessment?

Yes, most assessments allow unlimited retakes. Your highest score is recorded. Some compliance exams have a cooldown period between attempts.

Who do I contact for technical issues?

Email academy@airpay.co.in with a screenshot and description of the issue. The L&D team responds within 24 hours on business days.
