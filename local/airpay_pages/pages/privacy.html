1. Introduction

Airpay Payment Services Pvt Ltd ("Airpay", "we", "us") operates Airpay Academy, a learning management platform. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, employee ID, department, designation
  • Learning Data: Course enrolments, progress, completion status, assessment scores, certificates earned
  • Usage Data: Login timestamps, pages visited, time spent on courses, device and browser information
  • Communication Data: Messages, forum posts, feedback submissions

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the learning platform
  • To track learning progress and generate compliance reports
  • To issue certificates and badges upon course completion
  • To send notifications about deadlines, new courses, and platform updates
  • To generate aggregated analytics for organisational learning insights

4. Data Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+), access controls based on role and department, and regular security audits. Your data is stored on secure servers within India in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. For any data-related queries, contact us at academy@airpay.co.in.

6. Data Retention

Learning records are retained for the duration of your employment plus 7 years for compliance purposes. Account data is deleted within 90 days of account deactivation.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via platform notification.
