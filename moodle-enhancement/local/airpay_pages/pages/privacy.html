How airpay academy collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Airpay Payment Services Pvt Ltd operates airpay academy, a learning management platform. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email, employee ID, department, designation
  • Learning Data: Course enrolments, progress, completion status, assessment scores, certificates
  • Usage Data: Login timestamps, pages visited, time on courses, device and browser info
  • Communication Data: Messages, forum posts, feedback submissions

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the learning platform
  • To track learning progress and generate compliance reports
  • To issue certificates and badges upon course completion
  • To send notifications about deadlines, new courses, and updates
  • To generate aggregated analytics for organisational learning insights

4. Data Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+), role-based access controls, and regular security audits. Data is stored on secure servers within India in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023.

5. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Right of Access (Section 11): Request a copy of all your personal data
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure (Section 12): Request deletion of your account and data
  • Right to Grievance Redressal (Section 13): Raise complaints with the Data Protection Officer

Exercise Your Rights: Use the My Privacy & Data self-service portal to download your data or request account deletion. For other queries, contact the Data Protection Officer at academy@airpay.co.in. Response time: 72 hours for data access, 30 days for deletion.

6. Data Retention

Learning records are retained for the duration of employment plus 7 years for compliance. Account data is deleted within 90 days of deactivation.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated via platform notification.
