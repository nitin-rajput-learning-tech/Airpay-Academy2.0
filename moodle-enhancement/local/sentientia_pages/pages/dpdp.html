Your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)

DPDP Act 2023 Compliant

1. About This Notice

Airpay Academy ("we", "us") operates as a Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). This notice explains how we collect, process, and protect your personal data, and your rights as a Data Principal.

2. Data We Collect

When you create an account or use our platform, we collect:

  • Identity Data: Name, email address, mobile number
  • Learning Data: Course enrollments, completions, quiz scores, certificates
  • Usage Data: Login timestamps, page views, learning duration
  • Device Data: Browser type, IP address (for security purposes)

3. Lawful Purpose (Section 4)

We process your data for the following lawful purposes:

  • Providing and improving the learning platform
  • Tracking your course progress and issuing certificates
  • Sending learning reminders and notifications (with your consent)
  • Compliance with regulatory requirements applicable to financial services training

For employees, data processing is based on the employment contract. For external/public users, processing is based on your consent at registration.

4. Your Rights (Sections 11-14)

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

Right to Access (Section 11)
Request a copy of all your personal data stored on this platform.
Right to Correction (Section 11)
Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
Right to Erasure (Section 12)
Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
Available to external/public users only. Employee data governed by employment contracts.
Right to Grievance Redressal (Section 13)
File complaints with our Data Protection Officer if your rights are not respected.

5. How to Exercise Your Rights

External/Public users: Visit My Privacy & Data to download your data or request account deletion.

Employees: Your data is governed by employment contracts (Section 4, lawful purpose). Contact your HR team or the DPO for data correction requests.

Data Protection Officer: academy@airpay.co.in

Response Times: 72 hours for data access requests, 30 days for deletion requests.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest
  • Role-based access controls with multi-tenant data isolation
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure hosting on AWS infrastructure within India

7. Data Retention

  • Learning records: Retained for the duration of your account plus 7 years for compliance
  • Account data: Deleted within 90 days of account deletion request
  • Certificates: Retained permanently for verification purposes (anonymized after deletion)
  • Employee data: Retained for duration of employment plus 7 years per applicable labour laws
