## 4. Finance explainer

Source: the finance cluster. Airpay Finance was not consulted; this text exists so Nitin can take the questions to Finance.

**What a BizLMS credit balance is.** The old shopping cart can hold money "on account" for a learner. A balance is created in only three ways: an admin cancels a paid booking and gives the money back as credit instead of to the card; an admin adds or corrects credit by hand; or a learner rebooks and the cancellation fee is returned as credit. Each change is written as one line in a running list per learner, with a matching line in the cart's cash book, and the newest line holds the balance. The learner can later spend it at checkout, or an admin can pay it back in cash or by bank transfer.

**What the April copy of production holds.** Nothing. The credit list and the cash book are both empty: no learner has a balance, the total is Rs 0 (the cart's only currency is INR), so there is no oldest or newest balance. The cart was barely used: five orders in fifteen months, all by Public learners. One was paid (Rs 10, 10 January 2025); four never finished paying (Rs 2,197 attempted). None charged GST.

**What the ERPNext invoices are.** After a paid checkout, BizLMS can send the order to an outside accounting system called ERPNext, which writes the sales invoice. BizLMS keeps only ERPNext's invoice number next to its own order number. BizLMS never numbers or prints a tax invoice itself (it can print a booking receipt), and the Airpay payment page has no invoice feature either. On the April copy, ERPNext was never connected (no address, key or invoicing setting) and there are no stored invoice numbers. The one Rs 10 sale has no invoice anywhere.

**The GST rule.** A legal tax invoice must come from the seller's registered invoicing system, carry a number that is unique for the financial year, and never be issued twice for one sale. So Sentientia must not create new invoice numbers for old BizLMS sales, and must not present a copied number as its own invoice.

**The choices.** For balances: (1) freeze them as history that only admins can see, untouched until Finance acts; (2) let learners spend them in Sentientia; (3) pay them out; (4) cancel them at import. Choices 2 to 4 move or cancel money someone may be owed and cannot be undone, and choice 2 needs a spending feature Sentientia does not have. For invoices: (1) copy each ERPNext number as a reference ("Issued in ERPNext as ..."), for admins only; (2) issue new Sentientia invoices for old sales, which GST does not allow; (3) link to ERPNext, which does not exist to link to; (4) leave them behind, which hides them from Finance if any appear.

**Recommendation.** Freeze the balances and copy invoice numbers as references only, leaving the official invoice wherever Finance issued it. With today's data nothing changes for anyone. If the final live backup brings any balance or invoice, it arrives frozen and becomes visible to admins only once you switch on the history pages. Nothing is paid, spent, cancelled or invoiced.

**Self-review.** The strongest objection: a frozen balance could sit forever while a learner is owed money, and keeping this machinery for empty tables adds complexity. It does not win: the code is already built and tested, costs nothing when the tables are empty, and is the only choice that leaves every later Finance answer open. To stop "frozen" turning into "forgotten", the rehearsal report must list any balance or invoice, and you send that list to Finance before cutover.
