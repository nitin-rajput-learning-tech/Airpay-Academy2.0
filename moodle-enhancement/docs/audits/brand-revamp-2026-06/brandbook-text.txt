make more happen.

===== PAGE 6 =====
Consistency in applications plays a major 
part in building a solid brand. An 
underlying system provides flexibility and 
variety but at the same time holds 
everything together and helps to create 
ownable visual signatures over a course of 
time through a consistent look and feel.
All the elements like the logo, bounding 
shapes, typography, lock-ups, etc. are ﬁxed 
to the grid. This approach provides a clear 
starting point by leading with the logo and 
in turn generating proportions for the 
different elements making it easy to create 
layouts.

Social Media Exceptions:
When we talk about social media creatives we have 
a wider range of fonts and that cannot be penned 
down as we change or use fonts according to the to-
nality or concepts or language of the social media 
post contents.
But there are some protocols on using the font 
styles of a creative posted on social media, below 
are the rules and protocols
maintained -
Headlines: Straight, Bold fonts can be serif or 
san-serif or
cursive font styles.
Body: san-serif clean fonts not bold, unless required.
CTA: Bold san-serif fonts used for body text.

===== PAGE 14 =====
Colour theory Exceptions:
we prefer to keep a wider reach when we talk about colours and here at airpay we chose to keep it subtle and classy, as mentioned above the “airpay blue” is what we 
chose as a primary colour but we do have options of mixing primary colour with the secondary ones in a gradient format and tweak the colours to a shade-plus and a 
shade-minus when it calls for. Too much shade changes are not aprreciated as we do have our style signatures. Tints of blue and white are preffered with subtle
gradients of other colours mentioned above.  
Our customers recognise us by the colours 
that signify our company and ideologies.
Blue is the primary colour which portrays 
conﬁdence, stability, creativity, and 
inspiration. White is the primary colour 
representing the brand's core values which 
portrays innovation, success, and freedom. 
The white colour is recommended for usage 
in a smaller proportion than airpay blue.
