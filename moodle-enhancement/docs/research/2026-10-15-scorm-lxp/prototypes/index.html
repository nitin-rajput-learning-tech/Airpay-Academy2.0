Six clickable prototypes

One course, "Handling a disputed UPI transaction", seen from six seats: the learner who takes it, the author who builds it from an SOP, the reviewer who approves its translations, the admin who watches it, and the scenario designer who shapes its decisions. Everything is synthetic; nothing is live or saved.

Light and dark - LTR and RTL - 1440 and 375 px

How to read these

Each screen has an "About" button with what to click, the patterns it demonstrates, what it maps to in the real theme and what is deliberately not built. Colours, spacing, type, motion and focus all come from assets/tokens.css, which mirrors the theme's token names, so a component lifts into theme/sentientia unchanged. Open from the file system in Chrome or Edge; no server and no build. Montserrat loads from Google Fonts if online; otherwise system fonts are used.

URL switches for review: ?theme=dark, ?dir=rtl, ?nofonts=1.
