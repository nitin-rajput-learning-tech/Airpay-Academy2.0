# Capability matrix - scoring method

**Scale (per criterion).** 0 absent or no public evidence; 1 partial, or claimed only in the vendor's own marketing without documentation; 2 shipped and documented in help-centre or documentation pages, or confirmed by an independent source; 3 shipped and clearly differentiated, independently confirmed. Never 3 on marketing alone; err low when evidence is thin. A zero means "no evidence found", which is a research gap, not proof of absence.

**Criteria.** 51 criteria in nine clusters (`criteria.json`): A ingestion and generation (1.0), B assessment (0.8), C scenarios and roleplay (1.0), D localisation and review (1.2), E speech and captions (1.0), F standards and packaging (1.2), G delivery, mobile and offline (0.8), H reinforcement and analytics (0.9), I enterprise and trust (1.2). Weights reflect the buyer profile chosen on 2026-10-15 (India BFSI first, global in parallel): standards, localisation, compliance and review governance weigh more than social features. Totals are weighted percentages of the maximum.

**Vendors.** 20 platforms: the report's five (KREDO/Prodient, Tenneo, Invince, Mindsmith, Coursebox), five more in the deep tier (Docebo, 360Learning, Moodle Workplace, Sana, Articulate 360) and ten in the light tier (Cornerstone, Degreed, SAP SuccessFactors Learning, Workday Learning, LinkedIn Learning Hub, Absorb, TalentLMS, Elucidat/Create, Synthesia, 7taps). Each was scored by the researcher who studied it (`vendor-scores/R1-scores.json`, `R2a-scores.json`, `R2b-scores.json`, 20 words of evidence per cell) with a confidence level: low for 10 vendors, medium for 10, none high. Criteria that do not apply to a product category (for example quizzes in a video tool) are scored 0 with a note.

**Sentientia.** Scored from code and state cards at three points: today; after Stage 1 of the plan (day 90); after Stage 3 (day 365). The last two are targets and must not be read as facts. Alpha and flag-off features score 1 or 2, never 3. F1 and F2 for Sentientia today count what Moodle's `mod_scorm` plays (SCORM 1.2 verified end to end, 2004 with partial sequencing); the content engine's own output is the demo packager with known runtime flaws.

**Known biases.** Sentientia is scored with full visibility into its code; vendors from public pages, where review sites (G2, Capterra) refused automated access and the fetch tool returned summaries. Two vendors (Sana, 360Learning) rest mostly on search summaries. Some Moodle core scores rest on the researcher's own knowledge and are flagged in the score file. The structure of the findings (open lanes in localisation governance, in-region speech and decision analytics; India residency unclaimed by every vendor) survives a one-point change in any single cell; the exact percentages do not.

**Rebuilding.** `python -I tools/merge_scores.py matrix`, then `python -I tools/build_matrix.py matrix/criteria.json matrix/vendors.json matrix/capability-matrix.csv matrix/cluster-totals.md`.
