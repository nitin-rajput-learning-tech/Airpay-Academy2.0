## 1. Method and sources

**Inputs.** (a) The report, extracted with a stdlib/defusedxml script (`tools/extract.py`) from the .docx and read in full; it carries leftover `[span_NNN]` citation markers typical of an AI research export and no hyperlinks, so each of its 86 "works cited" entries was treated as a lead, not as evidence. (b) The Sentientia repository on `claude/gap-integration` at 7366c6050: `CLAUDE.md`, `PROJECT-STATE.md`, the ADR index and ADR-001/005/012/016/018/028/033, the 47 plugins under `moodle-enhancement/local/`, the two pipeline trees (`sentientia/`, `scripts/agents/`), `theme/sentientia`, the business and competitive folders, the maturity audit of 2026-08-04 and the signed decision memo. (c) Six web-research streams run in parallel on 2026-10-15 (vendors named in the report; the deep and light LMS/authoring set; open-source TTS/ASR and India GPU pricing; standards and India compliance; enterprise UX patterns; learning-science evidence), each delivered as a cited note in `sources/`.

**Scoring.** The capability matrix (§4, `matrix/capability-matrix.csv`) uses 0–3 per criterion (0 absent, 1 partial or unverified claim, 2 shipped, 3 shipped and differentiated) across nine clusters: document ingestion and generation; question types and assessment; branching scenarios and roleplay; localisation and human review; speech and captions; standards and packaging; delivery, mobile and offline; reinforcement and analytics; enterprise and trust. Weights follow the buyer profile Nitin chose (India BFSI first): standards, localisation, compliance and review governance weigh more than social features. Sentientia is scored from code at two points — today, and after Stage 1 of the plan — so the plan's effect is visible.

**UX benchmark.** Six surfaces (learner home, course player, authoring studio, localisation review, admin analytics, scenario editor/player) were benchmarked from public product pages, help centres and reviews, distilled into patterns to adopt and avoid (§10), and turned into clickable HTML prototypes on the real design tokens (`prototypes/`). A ten-criterion rubric scores the market and the prototypes consistently.

**Validation.** The architecture was proven by a reference implementation: the pilot harness compiles the canonical course JSON to SCORM 1.2, SCORM 2004 4th Edition and cmi5 packages, validates them and runs in mock mode with zero spend (§13). Costs use list prices captured on 2026-10-15 and current Claude API prices (Sonnet 5.5 $2/$10, Sonnet 4.6 $3/$15, Opus 5.5 $4/$20, Haiku 5.5 $0.10/$0.50 per million tokens).

**What was not done.** No live model, TTS or translation call; no Moodle upload of the pilot packages (Moodle runtime testing is Stage 1 work on UAT, not on this workstation); no Figma/Stitch; no changes to plugin code.
