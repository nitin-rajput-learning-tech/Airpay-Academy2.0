# External Public Learner — User Guide

!!! info "Who this guide is for"
    You are an **external learner** — typically an employee of another Indian fintech, or an individual practitioner — who pays for course access on the Airpay Academy Public tenant (`/77`). You are **not** an Airpay employee. This is the commercial side of the platform.

## Your feature set at a glance

From Section 10.8 of the Master Technical Doc (12 May 2026):

> Restricted feature set scoped to the Public tenant: catalogue and course detail (Public-only categories), self-enrolment for free Public-tenant courses, cart and checkout for paid courses, payment via Airpay gateway, invoice download (GST-compliant), profile edit, certificates, support contact.

## What's new since v1

**The entire flow is new.** v1 had no commercial tenant. Cart, checkout, payment, invoice, and refund self-service are all v2 capabilities, all shipped between 11 and 12 May 2026.

## What's still missing

- Per-tenant SSO (your organisation cannot yet plug in its own identity provider — you sign in with Airpay-issued credentials)
- Self-service organisation management for tenant administrators inside the Public tenant
