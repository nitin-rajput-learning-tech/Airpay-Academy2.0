# Course Author / Subject Matter Expert — User Guide

!!! info "Who this guide is for"
    You are a **subject-matter expert** in a functional team (Product, Engineering, Sales, Customer Support, Finance, Operations) who builds and maintains specific courses for your domain. You do not need the full L&amp;D Administrator surface.

!!! warning "Role status — Phase 9 backlog"
    Currently the Course Author role overlaps with [L&amp;D Administrator](../03-ld-administrator/index.md). A dedicated `course_author` role that scopes capabilities tightly to course-context editing (without exposing organisation-level administration) is scheduled in Phase 9 of the backlog. Until then, use the L&amp;D Administrator guide and only exercise the course-creation features.
